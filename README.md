# hey, i'm tarang

> stats + cs @ uiuc · curious about the systems underneath

I'm a junior at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign**, studying **Statistics + Computer Science** with a minor in Mathematics. I like building things that help me understand how models run, how data moves, and how systems hold up when something breaks.

A lot of my interests sit between **AI/ML, infrastructure, and scientific computing**. I enjoy the modeling side, but I'm just as curious about everything needed to make an experiment run well: the data pipeline, the inference engine, the containers, and the machines underneath.

Outside of that: squirrel and bird watching, and probably another side project.

<img src="assets/paper-plane.gif" width="180" alt="A small paper airplane gently glides along a dotted path." />

### things i keep coming back to

- **ML systems & inference** — how models get served, where the bottlenecks are, and how to evaluate changes properly. Especially interested in LLM infrastructure and efficient inference.
- **Data engineering** — turning messy data into useful datasets and building pipelines that are easier to understand, query, and maintain.
- **Distributed systems & cloud** — consensus, worker coordination, failure recovery, and what happens when the happy path stops being happy.
- **Scientific computing & HPC** — parallel workloads, numerical methods, reproducible experiments, and making compute useful for science.
- **Open source** — reading unfamiliar code, finding something small to improve, and learning through the process.
